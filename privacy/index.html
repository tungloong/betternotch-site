Privacy Policy

This policy covers the BetterNotch Mac app and this public website. The app keeps your settings on your Mac and does not require an account.

On this page

  • Data collection
  • Local information
  • System permissions
  • This website
  • Policy updates
  • Contact

Data collection

The BetterNotch Mac app does not collect, upload, or transmit personal data, usage data, or diagnostic data.

The app contains no advertising, analytics tools, or tracking SDKs. It does not create user profiles, sell data, or share data with third parties.

Information stored locally

To remember your preferences, the app stores the following in its local container on your Mac:

  • Per-display appearance and blend choices, including remembered values while Black is selected or blending is paused
  • Related detail settings such as blend width, curve, and glass end shapes
  • Whether the app icon is shown in the menu bar, the Dock, or neither

Launch at Login is managed by macOS after you turn it on. These app settings remain only on your Mac and are not sent to the developer or any third party. They remain until replaced by newer settings or the app’s local container is deleted.

System permissions

BetterNotch uses public macOS APIs to draw the menu bar effect from display geometry and your settings. It does not read your wallpaper.

The app does not request Screen Recording permission and does not read or record the windows or screen content of other apps.

This website

The public BetterNotch website is separate from the Mac app. In your browser it stores only the language you select, so the choice persists between pages and visits. Choosing a look on the homepage is temporary page state and is not kept after a reload. None of these website preferences is sent to the developer.

The public website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not a visitor is signed in. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for details. This site does not add analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, or third-party scripts.

Policy updates

If a future version changes how data is handled, we will update this Privacy Policy and the App Store privacy disclosure before releasing that version.
