Help and issue reporting

Support

BetterNotch requires macOS 26 or later. Its purpose is to let the physical notch disappear into the menu bar. Gradient is the signature look; Liquid Glass, Ink blending, and a fully black appearance are additional choices. Each display can be set independently.

Before you begin

If the system menu bar background is covering BetterNotch, open System Settings → Menu Bar and turn off “Show menu bar background.”

Accessibility → Display → Reduce Transparency can also cover the effect with an opaque menu bar. The app may point you to System Settings so you can decide. It does not change accessibility settings for you, and you do not need to turn off features you rely on.

Choose a look

Open BetterNotch. Everything is in the main window, in three steps:

  1. Choose a display.
  2. Menu bar appearance: Default, Liquid Glass, or Black.
  3. Blend the notch: None, Standard, or Ink. Displays without a notch keep this step visible but disabled. You can still change appearance, including glass end shapes.

Default and Liquid Glass both work with Standard or Ink. Black is its own appearance and pauses blending. Default with None restores that display’s original look.

If the effect does not appear

  1. Open BetterNotch and select the intended screen under Choose a display.
  2. On a notched display, choose Standard or Ink under Blend the notch, or Liquid Glass or Black under Menu bar appearance. Displays without a notch support appearance only.
  3. Default appearance with None selected for blending keeps the original look. Check any system-setting guidance in the window.
  4. Switch to another appearance, then return to your previous choice.
  5. If the problem continues, quit and reopen BetterNotch.

Compare before and after

Hold the comparison control at the top right of the main window to temporarily see the selected display’s original appearance. Release to restore the effect. Comparing does not change your saved settings, and it applies only to the current display.

Keyboard and accessibility activation uses a toggle instead of hold. Comparison ends when the control deactivates or the window is no longer active.

Launch at Login

To start BetterNotch when you log in:

  1. Click Settings at the top right of the main window. Settings opens inside the same window; there is no separate settings window.
  2. Turn on Launch at Login.
  3. If macOS says approval is required, approve BetterNotch in Login Items in System Settings, then return to the app.

Login launch restores your effects. It does not bring BetterNotch to the front or open the main window.

App icon

In Settings, choose whether the app icon appears in the menu bar, in the Dock, or in neither. Effects keep running when both entry points are hidden. Open BetterNotch from Applications to return to the main window.

Restore the original look

Select the display and choose Default under Menu bar appearance. On a notched display, also choose None under Blend the notch. Quitting BetterNotch removes its effects from every display.

Contact support

If the issue is still unresolved, email longbuild@icloud.com and include as much of the following as possible:

  • Your Mac model and macOS version
  • The currently selected effect
  • Your built-in and external display configuration
  • The steps you took before the problem appeared
  • A screenshot if needed, with personal information redacted first

We will do our best to help you identify the cause.
